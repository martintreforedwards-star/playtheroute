A Note About The Route

The practical bits, explained in plain English.
🚉 The Route is a puzzle game, not a journey planner.

Using The Route

The Route is a puzzle game created for entertainment purposes only.

While every effort is made to ensure that puzzle content is accurate and consistent, we cannot guarantee that all information, clues, station data, route details, or journey times are free from error.

By using this website, you acknowledge that all puzzles, clues, scores, and gameplay outcomes are provided for entertainment purposes only.

Journey Times & Data

Some puzzle clues and gameplay mechanics are derived from publicly available railway information, including timetable and journey-time data.

Where journey times are used within puzzles, they may represent canonical game values chosen for consistency and gameplay purposes and should not be relied upon for real-world travel planning.

Although real railway locations, routes, stations, and journey times may be used as inspiration for puzzles, gameplay may simplify, modify, or reinterpret such information for puzzle design purposes.

Puzzle Decisions

Where multiple valid routing interpretations exist, The Route's published solutions, rules, and canonical data set shall be considered authoritative for gameplay purposes.

Feedback

Feedback, corrections, and suggestions are always welcome and help us improve The Route. While we carefully consider all comments received, not every suggestion will make it onto the timetable.

Decisions regarding puzzle design, rules, scoring, and canonical data remain at the discretion of The Route's creators.
