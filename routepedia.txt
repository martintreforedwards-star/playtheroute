<h2>🛤️ How Routepedia defines routes</h2>

<p>
Routepedia is built from operational railway data rather than manually
maintained route lists.
</p>

<p>
The Route analyses scheduled train services from the Rail Delivery Group's
Darwin timetable feed and automatically derives canonical routes from the
ordered sequence of stations served by each train.
</p>

<p>
This means Routepedia reflects how Britain's railways actually operate.
As timetables evolve, routes can be regenerated from the latest operational
data, reducing manual maintenance and ensuring the network remains current.
</p>

<p>
Some well-known branded routes may also be shown using their commonly
recognised names, but these are presentation labels applied to the
underlying operational routes derived from timetable data.
</p>
