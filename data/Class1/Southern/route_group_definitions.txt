# Southern Route Groups

## Brighton Main Line

The principal Southern corridor linking London Bridge and London Victoria with Gatwick Airport, Haywards Heath, Brighton and intermediate stations.

## East Coastway

The coastal corridor linking Brighton with Lewes, Eastbourne, Hastings and Ore.

## West Coastway

The coastal corridor linking Brighton with Worthing, Chichester, Havant, Portsmouth Harbour and Southampton Central.

## Arun Valley

The inland corridor linking Horsham with Arundel, Ford, Barnham and the South Coast.

## Portsmouth Direct

The corridor linking London Victoria with Guildford, Haslemere, Petersfield, Havant and Portsmouth Harbour.

## Sutton & Metro

The suburban Southern network serving South London, Sutton, Epsom, Crystal Palace, West Croydon and associated metro corridors.

## East Grinstead Branch

The branch linking East Croydon with Oxted, Lingfield and East Grinstead.

## Uckfield Branch

The branch linking East Croydon with Oxted, Crowborough, Buxted and Uckfield.

## Thameslink Core

The central Thameslink corridor between London Bridge, Blackfriars, City Thameslink, Farringdon and St Pancras International.

## Southern Thameslink Branches

Southern-operated Thameslink routes south of the core, including services via Brighton, Horsham, Three Bridges, Sevenoaks and Sutton.
