How You Can Get Involved

Help keep future journeys on track.
🚉 The Route is built independently and powered by its community.

The Route is independently created and maintained by rail enthusiasts who love puzzles, stations, and the stories hidden within Britain's railway network.

If you've enjoyed playing and would like to support the project, there are several ways you can help.

☕ Buy us a coffee

The simplest way to support The Route is through Ko-fi.

Every contribution helps support:

  • Website hosting and running costs
  • New commuter networks
  • Additional puzzles and content
  • Routepedia development
  • Future features and improvements
  • Keeping The Route free to play
☕ Support The Route on Ko-fi

📣 Spread the word

Tell a friend, share a score, post a screenshot, or mention The Route on social media. Word of mouth is one of the best ways to help independent projects grow.

💡 Share ideas and feedback

Have an idea for a feature, a new commuter network, a station fact for Routepedia, or a puzzle improvement?

We'd love to hear from you. Every message helps us make The Route better.

🚉 Keep travelling with us

The biggest compliment of all is simply coming back tomorrow for another puzzle.

Thank you for travelling with us and helping keep The Route running.
