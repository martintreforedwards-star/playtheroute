# Region Definitions

## Purpose

Regions provide a geographical classification for stations within The Route.

Regions are used for:

* Clue generation
* Routepedia enrichment
* Gameplay variety
* Station grouping

Regions should be recognisable to most players and avoid excessive granularity.
