# ScotRail Network Scope

## Purpose

Defines the station coverage for the ScotRail network within *The Route*.

The ScotRail network represents the principal passenger railway across Scotland, combining intensive suburban commuter services, inter-city corridors, rural main lines, remote Highland routes and some of Britain's most scenic branch railways.

The network offers an exceptional mix of urban, regional and rural stations, providing highly varied gameplay with distinctive route identities, long-distance journeys and numerous interchange opportunities.

## Exclusions

The following are excluded from this network:

* Caledonian Sleeper-only operations
* London North Eastern Railway services beyond ScotRail-operated stations
* CrossCountry-only stations outside the ScotRail network
* Freight-only infrastructure
* Heritage railways
* Glasgow Subway
* Edinburgh Trams
* Light rail and metro systems

Stations shared with other operators may appear in multiple game datasets where appropriate.

## Gameplay Characteristics

The network is characterised by:

* Dense commuter services around Glasgow
* Busy commuter network around Edinburgh
* Long-distance inter-city routes
* Remote Highland railways
* Scenic branch lines
* Coastal destinations
* Strong regional identities
* Large geographical variation
* Significant differences in service frequency
* Numerous interchange opportunities
