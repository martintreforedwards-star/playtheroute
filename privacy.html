Privacy

How we handle information on The Route.
🔒 Your privacy matters. The Route is designed to be simple, enjoyable, and respectful of your data.

What information do we collect?

The Route does not require you to create an account or provide personal information in order to play.

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message.

Local Storage

The Route may use your browser's local storage to remember gameplay preferences such as:

  • Your selected commuter network
  • Your chosen game mode
  • Other gameplay preferences

This information is stored on your device and is not used to identify you.

Analytics

The Route uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to help us understand how visitors use the website and improve the player experience.

Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregated information such as page views, device types, and general usage patterns. It is designed to be privacy-friendly and does not use cross-site tracking to identify individual visitors.

Third-Party Links

The Route may contain links to third-party websites, including social media platforms and support services such as Ko-fi.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

Updates
